Exploit Chain Reproduction Tested exploit_chain_poc.py (OOM producer + 8 pipelined consumers) on local 8.4.26 and 8.4.27 FPM containers. Heap leak did not trigger. Full reproduction requires specific Alpine CLI-server SAPI (PHP_CLI_SERVER_WORKERS=4) and musl memory layout.
Environment Sensitivity Debian PHP-FPM worker recycling and FastCGI buffering prevent HTTP pipelining connection reuse needed for the race condition. Behavior of multi-worker FPM under non-buffered raw unix domain socket connections.
